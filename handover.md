- 5th country added: `philippines/` (10 pages, hub + filing + FEIE + tax treaty & no totalization + dual citizens & balikbayan + retiring/SRRV + checklist + teachers + condo ownership 40% rule + digital nomad visa). Philippines' standout issue is different from every other country so far: a large share of "American expats" here are actually Filipino-American dual citizens or balikbayan returnees who don't realize citizenship-based US taxation still applies to them, that got its own dedicated page rather than being folded into the hub. Also distinctive: a real 1982 tax treaty exists (unlike Oman/Saudi) but no Totalization Agreement (same SE tax trap as the Gulf countries anyway), foreigners constitutionally cannot own land (condo-only, capped at 40% foreign ownership per building under RA 4726), and the SRRV retirement visa deposit is itself a reportable FBAR/FATCA account many retirees overlook. Because Philippine tax brackets only hit 35% above ~$142k USD, FEIE (not FTC) is the primary tool here, opposite of Australia. ## Session Log
- 07/14/2026 | Fixed reported mobile bugs across all 3 countries (Oman screenshots as the diagnostic starting point, root causes turned out to be global). Real root causes, in the order found: (1) `.callout-box` inline `padding: 0.8rem 160px` squeezing body text to one word per line, (2) `.footer-top-inner`'s `grid-auto-flow: column` never reset on mobile so the footer kept generating columns instead of stacking, (3) `homepage.css`/`thailand.css` cascade order silently re-showing the hero image on country pages despite `homepage.css`'s own hide rule, (4) hero photo still downloaded on mobile at `fetchpriority="high"` despite being CSS-hidden, fixed via `<picture>` + media-gated source, (5) GSAP hero text fade-in (~1.3s delay) forced to instant on mobile for LCP. Then rebuilt the mobile nav entirely: the old `#nav-mobile-menu` was a separate, hand-duplicated flat hub-only link list (no sub-pages reachable on mobile at all, country taps navigated straight to the hub instead of expanding) that had also drifted out of sync content-wise across pages. Deleted it site-wide and made `.nav-links` itself the mobile menu (CMA/chiangmaiambassador's pattern: one markup tree, full-screen accordion below 1024px, `max-height`-based expand/collapse, click handlers with `e.preventDefault()` gated to mobile width). Found and fixed two landmines this surfaced: `css/pages.css` had its own fully independent, older copy of every nav rule that would've silently overridden the fix via cascade order on the 8 `/pages/*.html` articles (deleted the duplicate), and 7 of those same articles had dead inline `<script>` blocks referencing the now-deleted `#nav-mobile-menu` that would've thrown on burger click (removed). Two more scroll-related bugs found after initial "done": `#nav.scrolled`'s `backdrop-filter` was making `#nav` a containing block for its `position: fixed` descendants (`.nav-links`), so the mobile menu rendered behind page content once scrolled past 50px (fixed by dropping the nav bar's own blur while the menu is open); and `#anchor` links landed their target flush behind the fixed nav bar with no offset (fixed with a global `[id] { scroll-margin-top: calc(var(--nav-h) + 1rem) }`). `md/playbook-new-country.md` updated to match all of the above so the next country build doesn't reintroduce any of it. 12 commits total, all pushed to `main`.

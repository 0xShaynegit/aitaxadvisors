What is the FBAR and do I need to file it?

The FBAR (FinCEN Form 114) reports Foreign Bank and Financial Accounts. U.S. citizens with foreign accounts exceeding USD 10,000 in aggregate must file. We handle full FBAR compliance and can help you catch up on delinquent filings through government amnesty programs.

What is the Foreign Earned Income Exclusion (FEIE)?

The FEIE allows qualifying U.S. expats to exclude foreign earned income (up to USD 120,000+ annually) from U.S. taxation. We optimise your FEIE strategy to minimise your overall tax burden while remaining fully compliant.

Do U.S. expats in Thailand pay taxes on worldwide income?

Yes, the U.S. taxes citizens on worldwide income regardless of residence. However, treaties, exclusions and foreign tax credits can substantially reduce or eliminate liability. Our advisors navigate both U.S. and Thai tax law to optimise your position.

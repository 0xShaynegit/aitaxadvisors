Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

American International Tax Advisers (“AIT,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) provides U.S. tax preparation, planning, and advisory services to Americans living abroad and cross-border businesses. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

Information you provide directly: When you contact us by email, phone, or through a consultation request, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, and details about your tax situation. We do not collect Social Security Numbers, bank account details, or other sensitive financial documents through this website, those are only ever exchanged through secure channels once an engagement begins.

Information collected automatically: Like most websites, we use analytics tooling (Cloudflare Web Analytics) to understand aggregate visitor behavior, pages viewed, general location by country/region, device type, and referral source. This is privacy-respecting, cookieless analytics; it does not track you individually across other websites.

How We Use Information

  • To respond to consultation requests and inquiries you submit to us.
  • To provide the tax preparation and advisory services you engage us for.
  • To improve this website's content and understand which guides are most useful to visitors.
  • To comply with applicable legal, regulatory, and professional obligations.

How We Do Not Use Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes. Information you share with us in the course of a tax engagement is used solely for that engagement and is subject to the confidentiality standards applicable to tax preparers.

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Sensitive tax documents should only be exchanged through the secure channels we provide once an engagement is underway, not through general website contact forms or unencrypted email.

Third-Party Links

This website links to third-party resources, including the IRS and other government agencies, for reference purposes. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external sites.

Your Choices

You may contact us at any time to ask what information we hold about you, to request corrections, or to request deletion of information that is not required for us to retain under applicable tax, accounting, or legal recordkeeping obligations.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to admin@aitaxadvisers.com or our head office at 15/F Skyway Centre, 23 Queen's Road West, Sheung Wan, Hong Kong.
